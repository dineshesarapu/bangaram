Athamma…

Mee chethi chicken pulusu chaaaala ante chaaaala baagundi. ❤️

Kani aa roju meeru chesina chicken pulusu tinappudu enduko…

Naa manasuni evaro prema tho hattukunna feeling vachindi.

Aa chicken pulusu lo kevalam ruchi kaadhu…

Oka intlo manishi kosam vandi pettina prema undi.

Chala rojulu nundi naa jeevitham lo chaala vishayalu nene handle cheskuntu vachanu. Evariki dooramga, andariki dooramga, enni kashtalu vachina nene bharisthu… ishtapadi brathakadaniki prayatninchanu.

Konni saarlu entha strong ga unna…

“Naa pakkana evaraina unte baagundedi…” ani anipinche moments kuda unnayi.

Kani ippudu…

Nenu eppudu ontariga anipinchina, em kashtam vachina, naa Bangaram naa daggaraki vachi…

“Bava… nenu unna.” ani cheppinattu anipisthundi.

Nenu intini miss ayithe…

Athamma chethi special vanta dorukutundi.

Ammani miss ayithe…

Amma la naa Bangaram chupinche prema dorukutundi.

Nenu daari thappithe…

Naa Bangaram nanna la karra pattukoni daari chupisthundi. 😄❤️

Kani ee madhya naa Bangaram na meedha chupisthunna care…

Adi maatram naaku chala kothaga anipisthundi.

Naa jeevitham lo evaru nannu ala chusukunna feeling naaku eppudu raledu.

Anduke konni sarlu…

“Nijanga nenu inta care ki arhuda?” ani kuda anipisthundi.

Adi entha kothaga undho… Anthe andamga undi.

Swargam ante ekkado paina undadu emo…

Naa Bangaram undi.

“Entha pedda kashtam vachina sare… nenu gelustha.”

Naa manasu maatram chala rojula tarvatha poorthiga nindindi. ❤️

Bagundiiii Athamma… chaaaala baagundi.

Destiny & Faith

✨ Maybe This Is What Destiny Means… ❤️🕉️

Bangaram… Konni sarlu nenu alochisthunta… Destiny ante idhe emo.

Sai Baba manaki nijanga em kavalo, eppudu kavalo, ela ivvalo ayanake baga telusu ani antaru… Ee roju aa maata nijame anipisthundi.

Naa past lo jarigina konni sanghatanala valla… Nenu mellaga Sai Baba ni nammadam tagginchesanu. Namaskaram cheyyadam kuda almost manesanu.

Kani… Nuvvu naa jeevitham loki vachaka… Naa manasulo oka chinna korika puttindi. Oka roju… Nee cheyyi naa chethilo pattukoni… Sai Baba gudi ki vellali. Aayana mundhu nilabadi… “Baba… ee Bangaram ni ela aina naa jeevitham loki naa bharya ga teesukuraandi…” ani adagalani.

Kani appudu nuvvu… “Baba mandiram ki vasthe intlo telisipothundi…” annappudu… Enduko teliyani baadha. Konchem benga. Konchem bhayam. Aa roju naaku anipinchindi… Baba kuda manalni pilavaledemo ani.

Kani… Manam alochinchindhi okati… Aayana alochinchindhi inkokati.

Konni nelala tarvatha… Evaru uhinchani vidhamga… Ade Sai Baba… Maniddarini tana sannidhi daggaraki pilichadu.

Manam kalisi aayana kalla mundhu nilabadam… Naaku adhi oka coincidence la anipinchaledu. Adhi… Aayana pilupu la anipinchindi.

Aa roju nenu oka nammakam pondanu. Manam kalavadam… Manam kalisi Baba sannidhi ki velladam… Ivanni oorike jarigina sanghatanalu kaavu. Aayana rasina daari lo jarigina adugulu.

Appati nundi naa nammakam inkonchem balam ayyindi. Ee janmalo maatrame kaadhu… enni janmalu unna… ee Bava nee cheyyi vadaladu.

Endhukante… Sai Baba vadalanivvaru ani naa nammakam.

Om Sai Ram. ❤️🙏

🌿 Gorintaku & Our Future ❤️

Ee roju nuvvu gorintaku pettukunnav ani telisinappati nundi naa mind lo okate alochana tirugutundi… adi entha baaga panduthundo ani. Kani nijam cheppalante, adi ela pandina naa prema matram eppatiki taggadu. Ninnu life-long chala happy ga, muddu ga, jagrathaga chusukunta.

Chinnappudu maa mummy ki maa daddy gorintaku pettadam chusthe chala cute ga anipinchedi. Appudu andaroo oka maata cheppevaaru – “Gorintaku baaga pandithe manchi mogudu vasthadu.” Aa maata vinnappudalla, “Naa bharya ekkado untundi… eppudu naa life loki vasthundi?” ani alochinchevadni.

Ee roju aa kalaki roopam nuvve, naa Bangaram. ❤️

Ippudu naa manasulo oka korika maatrame undi… okaroju nenu nee chethullo gorintaku pettali. Nuvvu naa eduruga kurchoni navvuthu unte, nenu prati line ni prema tho geeyali. Aa gorintaku kanna ekkuvaga, naa prema nee chethullo nilichipovali.

Na jeevitham lo oka korika, mana pelli taruvatha nenu nee chethiki gorintaku pette aa andamaina roju kosam nenu veyi kallatho kaadu… laksha kallatho eduruchusthunna.

Gorintaku rangu konni rojule untundi… kani nee meeda naa prema matram ee janmaki kaadu, prati janmaki untundi.

Love you forever, naa Bangaram. 💜

Nuvvu navvuthu naa pakkana nilabade roju kosam nenu prathi roju poradatanu. Ee poratam mana iddaridhi… nuvvu matrame kaadu.

Aa mata vinnappudu nenu silent ga unna… kani na manasu lo matram oka promise chesukunna. Ela aina sare… enni kashtalu vachina sare… aa mata ni nenu tappakunda nijam chestha.

Bangaram… nijam cheppali ante, amma ki nuvvu ante chala ishtam. Kodalu ani maatladithe aavida kallallo kanipinche aanandam chusthe naaku chala emotional ga anipisthundi. Nuvvu gurinchi cheppina prathi sari aavidani face lo oka navvu untundi. Ninnu kalisina tharwataha aa ishtam inka ekkuva ayyindi.
