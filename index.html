Ninna Nenu Choosina Swargam… ❤️

Bangarammmmm…

Nenu chala rojula nundi anukunevadini…

Nuvvu naaku entha special o naaku telusu.
Manam kalisi gadipina prathi moment entha beautiful o kuda telusu.

Kani…

Ninna naaku vachina anandam ela untundo maatram nenu eppudu oohinchaledu.

Life lo chala enjoyments chesanu…

Long drives vellanu…
Bike rides chesanu…
Chala places chusanu…
Chala happy moments experience chesanu…

Kani ninna…

Nenu modatisari nijanga swargam chusanu.

Adi oka place kaadhu.

Adi oka moment kuda kaadhu.

Adi nuvvu nannu poorthiga nee lokam lo kalupukunnav ane feeling.

Ninna edho teliyani oka prapancham lo unnattu anipinchindi.

Manam iddaram veru veru manushulam ane feeling kuda oka kshanam ki ledu…

“Manam iddaram okkate” ane oka teliyani anubhuthi.

Nuvvu nannu nee lokam lo ila kalupukunnav ane feeling…

Adi naa kallalo ippatiki nundi povatam ledu.

Ninna nee daggara unna aa kshanalu gurthosthe…

Naa face meeda teliyakunda navvu vastundi.

Endhukante aa roju nenu kevalam oka beautiful moment ni experience cheyyaledu…

Naa Bangaram tho inka daggara ayyanane feeling ni experience chesanu.

Nuvvu naa jeevitham lo entha important o…

Ninna adi inka deep ga ardham ayyindi.

Thank you Bangarammmmm… ❤️

Naa life lo intha andamaina feeling ni ichinanduku.

Nannu nee lokam lo intha daggaraga chesinanduku.

Ninna naaku ichina aa anandam kosam…

Thank you for everything. ❤️

Kani…

Oka chinna complaint undi… 😜

Bangaram… inka kavali anipisthundi. 😂❤️

Ninna choopinchina aa swargam nachindhi…

Chaaaala nachindhi.

Kabatti ee Bava ki malli aa swargam ki entry pass eppudu isthav? 😜❤️

Entry pass okkasari ichesi lifetime membership lekunda pampinchesthe ee Bava oppukodu madam! 😂

Lifetime membership kavali… one-time experience tho satisfy avvanu! 😜❤️

Please consider my request and approve at the earliest. 😌😂

Endhukante…

Ninna oka sari choosina aa swargam kanna…

Nuvvu pakkana unte prathi roju ade swargam la undali anedhe naa korika. ❤️

Long drive aina…

Bike ride aina…

Oka chinna room lo iddaram maatladukovadam aina…

Naa Bangaram tho unte ade naa swargam. ❤️

Ninna oka kotha memory create ayyindi…

Kani naa korika okkate—

Aa memory ni mana jeevitham motham repeat cheskuntu, inka enno andamaina memories kalisi raasukundam. ❤️

I Love You, Bangarammmmm. 🫶❤️

Yours lovingly,
Mee konchem greedy Bava 😜

Naa Bangaram… Naa Lopala Oka Bhaagam ❤️

Enduko ee madhya Bangaram…

Nuvvu naaku daggaraga unnattu kaadhu… naalo okati aipothunnattu anipisthundi.

Mundu nuvvu naaku chala daggara ani anipinchedi. Kani ippudu… Nuvvu naalo oka bhaagam aipoyavemo anipisthundi. Leda… Nanne nee lopala kalipeskunnavemo.

Nee maatalu… Nee pogaru… Nee allari… Ivanni ippudu naaku chala garvanga anipisthunnayi. Ninnu chusthe… “Idi naa Bangaram” ani cheppukovalanipisthundi.

Nuvvu entha kashtam vachina navvuthu munduku vellipothav. Kani ade Bangaram… Bava konchem dooramga velthunte maatram chinna pillala edupu mokham peduthundi. 😄❤️

Enduko… Adi chusthe naaku chala mudduga anipisthundi. Bahusha… Nuvvu kuda nannu nee lopala kalupukunnavemo. Bahusha anduke Bava dooramga velthunte nee kallallo aa chinna baadha kanipisthundemo.

Naa muddula Bangaram… Naa Bangaru Konda… Nuvvu naaku ippudu oka person maatrame kaadhu. Naa jeevitham lo naatho kalisi nadiche oka bhaagam.

Nee santhosham naaku santhosham. Nee baadha naaku baadha. Nee gelupu naaku garvam. Nee bhayam naaku dhairyam ivvalanipinche reason. Nee navvu chusthe… Nenu inkem adagakunda happy aipotha.

Anduke naa pichi korika okkate… Ee roju nuvvu ela unnava… alane naa pakkana 60 years marriage anniversary roju kuda undali. ❤️

Appatiki mana juttu tellaga aipoyina… Mana nadaka slow aipoyina… Mana faces lo ee roju unna youth undakapoyina… Nuvvu Bava ki ade Bangaram ga undali.

Em vishayam aina… Enta chinna matter aina… Enta pedda problem aina… “Bava ki cheppali” ani anipinchela ninnu chuskovali. Nee manasulo unna prathi maata… Nee bhayam… Nee kopam… Nee allari… Nee korikalu… Anni Bava tho dhairyamga cheppagalige ammayiga undali.

Nuvvu naa pakkana unte… Nenu kuda prapancham motham edurukogalanu ane dhairyam naaku untundi.

Anduke… Naa Bangaram… Nuvvu naalo oka bhaagam aipoyavo… Leda nannu nee lopala kalipeskunnavo… Naaku teliyadu. Kani okati maatram telusu… Ee Bava ki ippudu “nenu” ane maata kanna “manam” ane maata chala ishtam. ❤️

  • ✨ Nuvvu navvuthu undali.
  • ✨ Nuvvu dhairyamga undali.
  • ✨ Nuvvu edaina naatho cheppagalagali.
  • ✨ Nuvvu eppudu… “Bava unnaadu” ane nammakam tho undali.

Ee pichi Bava korika anthe… Ee janma motham kaadhu Bangaram… mana 60th anniversary roju kuda nee cheyyi pattukoni, “Nuvvu appudu kuda na Bangaramve” ani cheppali. ❤️

Naa muddula Bangarammm…
Naa Bangaru Konda…
Nuvvu naalo kalisipoyav. ❤️
