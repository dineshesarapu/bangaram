Half Year Anniversary

Six Months… Yet It Feels Like I’ve Known You Forever. ❤️

Bangaram…

Eeroju mana journey ki 6 months.

Aru nelalu…

Cheppadaniki chinna number.

Kani aa aru nelallo nenu jeevitham motham gurthupettukune gnapakalu sampadinchanu.

Konni rojulu navvinchayi…
Konni rojulu edpinchayi…
Konni rojulu kopam theppinchayi…
Konni rojulu nammakam penchayi…
Kani oka roju kuda ninnu takkuvaga preminchaledu.

Ninnu kalisaka naaku ardham ayindi…

Prema ante pedda pedda gifts kaadhu… pedda pedda maatalu kaadhu… chinna chinna kshanalu.

  • Nee navvu chusthu coffee tagadam…
  • Nee cheyyi pattukoni road cross cheyyadam…
  • Restaurant lo neeku muddalu peduthu “inka konchem thinu” ani cheppadam…
  • Nee vesina painting lo nee manasuni choodadam…
  • Nee photo ni naa jeevitham lo first frame ga pettukovadam…
  • Nee Athamma chethi vanta tini amma gurthochi santhoshapadadam…
  • Nee chethullo gorintaku chusi naa future ni oohinchukovadam…
  • Sai Baba sannidhi lo iddaram kalisi nilabadadam…

Ivanni choosthe ardham avuthundi…

Mana prema pedda sanghatanalatho kaadhu… Chinna chinna gnapakalatho peruguthundani.

Nijam cheppalante…

Ee aru nelallo nuvvu naaku oka girlfriend maatrame kaaledu.

Naa best friend ayyavu. Naa dhairyam ayyavu. Naa navvu ayyavu.

Konni rojulu nannu baadha pettavu… Aa baadha nenu eppatiki marchipolenu.

Kani… Aa baadha kanna nuvvu naaku ichina santhosham chala ekkuva.

Anduke… Aa baadhani kuda oka gnapakam la dachukunna.

Endhukante… Adhi kuda mana journey lone oka page.

Nuvvu nannu marchesthunnaavu Bangaram…

Mundu nenu kalalu kannevadini. Ippudu mana future gurinchi kalalu kanthunnanu.

Mundu nenu naa gurinchi alochinchevadini. Ippudu mundhu nee gurinche alochisthanu.

Mundu nenu naa room lo naa photo frame pettukoledu. Ee roju naa room lo unna modati frame… Naa Bangaram.

Mundu Sai Baba ki namaskaram cheyyadam kuda manesanu. Ee roju… Mana iddarini tana sannidhi daggaraki pilichina aa Baba meedha malli nammakam puttindi.

Konni sarlu bhayam vesthundi… Future gurinchi. Paristhithula gurinchi.
Kani aa bhayam vachina prathi sari… Oka maata gurthosthundi.
Mana kalisina roju kuda manam plan cheyyaledu. Adhi jarigindi.
Appudu… Mana future ni kuda ayane rayagaladu ani nammutanu.

Bangaram… Thank you.

Nannu ardham chesukune prayatnam chesinanduku.
Naa allari bharinchinanduku.
Naa overthinking bharinchinanduku.
Naa prema ni oppukunnanduku.

Ee aru nelallo naaku ichina…

Prathi navvu… Prathi memory… Prathi photo… Prathi call… Prathi message… Prathi “Bava…”

Naa jeevitham lo atyanta viluvaina bahumathi.

Samayam gadichipothundi… Photos old aipothayi… Messages delete aipovachu…

Kani… Mana iddaram kalisi create chesina ee gnapakalu maatram eppatiki mana hrudayallo brathikuntayi.

Happy 6 Months, Bangaram. ❤️

Ee aru nelallo ninnu preminchina vidhanam kanna… Raboye prathi nela, prathi samvatsaram, prathi janmalo inka ekkuvaga preminchali anedhe naa korika.


Naa jeevitham lo jarigina ati andamaina coincidence… nuvvu.
